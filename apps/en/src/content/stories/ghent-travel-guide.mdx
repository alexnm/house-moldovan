## Introduction

Stuck between the bigger Brussels and the more renown Bruges, **Ghent** is a city that gives you the best of the two worlds. Don't skip it on your next trip to Belgium! You find less a less crowded version of Bruges, equally charming and a very compact and walkable old town.

Ghent has a long history of punching above its weight. It was once one of the most important cities in Europe, due to its strategic location as a trading hub.

<AssetFigure
  path="belgium/ghent-canal.JPG"
  alt="one of the main canals in Ghent."
  caption="One of the main canals in Ghent."
/>

Today, it is also a huge university center and is much more active during the evenings compared to Bruges. Young people gather around the terraces in the old town and on the banks of the canals, especially around the **Graslei** area.

## What to do

The old town of Ghent is quite compact and very walkable. You can spend some time roaming the streets, discovering cute houses with small shops and restaurants.

<AssetFigure
  path="belgium/ghent-houses.JPG"
  alt="Brick houses in the old town of Ghent."
  caption="Brick houses in the old town of Ghent."
/>

The symbol of the city is the 91m tall belfry located right next to the **St. Bavo Cathedral**. You can also visit the 10th century **Gravensteen castle** in the middle of the city, 10 minutes away from the belfry.

<AssetFigure
  path="belgium/ghent-graselai.JPG"
  alt="Graslei at sunset."
  caption="Graslei at sunset."
/>

The **Sint-Michielsburg** is also a great spot to take a snapshot before the sun goes down. It is a beautiful pedestrian bridge that offers a stunning view of the city's main sights. It's one of those places where you can lose track of time and just enjoy the atmosphere and the views.

<AssetFigure
  path="belgium/ghent-bridge.JPG"
  alt="The Sint-Michielsburg bridge in Ghent."
  caption="The Sint-Michielsburg bridge in Ghent."
/>

As the evening comes, make sure you grab a [Gentse Tripel](https://www.beeradvocate.com/beer/profile/48/12957/) or any other strong Belgian beer at one of the open terraces.

We spent only a day in Ghent during the same Interrail trip back in 2013, but we returned in 2025 and found the city to be even more charming than the first time.
