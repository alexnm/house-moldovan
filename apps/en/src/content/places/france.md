France needs no introduction. From the boulevards of Paris to the half-timbered towns of Alsace and the glamorous Côte d'Azur, it's a country that rewards slow travel, with great food and wine never too far away.

France is a country that requires multiple visits to truly appreciate its diversity. Paris is a destination in itself, one of the most visited cities in the world, but there is so much more to discover.

The south of the country is a perfect destination for a road trip, with beautiful beaches, charming hilltop villages and delicious food and wine.

The north-east part of the country, the historic regions of Alsace and Lorraine, are a perfect destination for a different type of travel. The region is known for its beautiful countryside, charming towns and an authentic mix of French and German culture.
