Jordan was part of our [10 day trip to the Middle East](/itineraries/israel-jordan-10-days) in early 2020. Unfortunately, since then, visiting the country has been difficult first due to the pandemic, then because of the instability in the region.

We can't say we're experts on the country, having been there just for two days during the [10 day trip to the Middle East](/itineraries/israel-jordan-10-days), but we absolutely loved the [Petra and Wadi Rum experience](/stories/petra-wadi-rum). Petra is every bit as monumental as the photographs suggest. But not just the famous Treasury, the entire complex and the surrounding landscape are worth exploring at your own pace.

Further south, the Wadi Rum desert gave us a taste of the nomadic life and the harsh and extreme conditions of the desert. Sleeping under the stars among the dunes and the rock mountains will be a long lasting memory.
