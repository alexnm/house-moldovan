The Netherlands is a small country with a big personality. Known for its flowers, bikes and canal cities, it is a great choice for a weekend trip or a long city hopping vacation.

There are many stereotypes and misconceptions about the Netherlands and while some of them are rooted in reality, others are just creating a false impression about the country. Yes, everyone uses bikes to move around, but not everyone smokes weed and parties all night long.

Amsterdam is not only about the coffee shops and the red light district. In fact, it is a city with a lot of history and culture, and a lot to offer to any kind of traveler, not just the party-goers.

As you move away from the big city, the Netherlands has a long list of charming cities and small canal towns ready to be discovered. Stop by Haarlem, Utrecht or Delft to get a taste of the Dutch lifestyle, without the crowds. Spring is the best time to visit the country, as the flowers are in full bloom and the weather is perfect.
