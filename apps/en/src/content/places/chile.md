The unique geography of Chile makes this one of the most diverse countries in the world in terms of landscape and climate. Spanning over 4,000km between the Pacific coast and the high Andes, Chile is a country of extremes, from dry deserts to lush forests and snow-capped peaks.

Our [trip to the southern cone](/itineraries/south-america-3-weeks) back in 2019 landed us in Santiago for a solid 5 days so we had plenty of time to explore the central part of the country. We had a great time exploring the high Andes as well as the port city of Valparaíso. We also crossed for a day to visit the magnificent Torres del Paine National Park, while staying in Argentina.

Chile is a country we look forward to visiting again, as there are numerous regions we haven't explored, like the Atacama Desert up north, the Lake District, and the remote national parks in the south.
