Snuggled up between the Netherlands, Germany and France, Belgium is a small country with a unique culture and history. Split in half between the Flemish and the Walloon regions, Belgium is known for its strong beer, delicious chocolate, and charming cities.

Bruges is one of the most picturesque cities in Europe with its beautiful old town, cobbled streets and brick houses. But if you plan on visiting Belgium, we recommend basing yourself in [Ghent](/stories/ghent-travel-guide), one of Europe's hidden gems.

In both our visits to Belgium, we skipped the capital city of Brussels as well as the southern Walloon region, but these are definitely on our radar for the next visit.
