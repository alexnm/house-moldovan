Snuggled up between the Netherlands, Germany and France, Belgium is a small country with a unique culture and history. Split in half between the Flemish and the Walloon regions, Belgium is known for its strong beer, delicious chocolate, and charming cities.

We keep remembering the phrase our guide at the De Halve Maan brewery in Bruges said: "It's like walking in a postcard". We visited countless cities in Europe, large and small towns alike, but for us Belgium has some of the most picture perfect streets and architecture.

Bruges also has a special significance for us as it is the city where we got engaged back in 2013. With its beautiful old town, cobbled streets and brick houses lining the canals, it is a must see for anyone visiting Belgium. But if you plan a longer trip, we recommend basing yourself in [Ghent](/stories/ghent-travel-guide), one of Europe's hidden gems.

In both our visits to Belgium, we skipped the capital city of Brussels as well as the southern Walloon region, but these are definitely on our radar for the next visit.
