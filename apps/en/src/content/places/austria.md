Austria is where imperial grandeur and alpine scenery sit just a few hours apart. Vienna and Salzburg still carry the weight of the Habsburg past, while the lakes, villages and high mountain passes of the Alps make it one of the best road trip countries in Europe.

Rich in history, art and nature, Austria is welcoming tourists all year round, with its famous Christmas markets, its impressive imperial cities and the breath-taking alpine scenery.

Cities like Vienna, Graz, Salzburg or Innsbruck each have their own charm and historical sights to offer. But make sure you venture outside the big cities and discover the picturesque villages, forests and lakes that cover a vast amount of the country.

We crossed the country on our [alpine road trip from Salzburg to Val Gardena](/itineraries/alpine-tour-austria-italy), with **Salzburg** as our base for the first days. The beautiful villages of Hallstatt and Gosau were two of the highlights of the trip while the most fun day was definitely the drive on the Grossglockner High Alpine Road.
