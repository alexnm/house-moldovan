Polish cities share a common style even if they historically had different influences and rulers. Market squares lined with narrow townhouses, brick churches rebuilt after war, facades painted in vibrantcolours. From Kraków's UNESCO-listed Old Town to Gdańsk's Hanseatic waterfront and Warsaw's painstakingly restored Old Town, the buildings tell the same story about resilience and renewal.
