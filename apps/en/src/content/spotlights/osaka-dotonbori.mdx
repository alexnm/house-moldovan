We only spent half a day in Osaka, while traveling through Kansai during our [cherry blossom trip](/itineraries/japan-cherry-blossom-2-weeks) in Japan. We went straight to the heart of the city to experience the urban chaos around Dōtonbori.

This is one of the most vibrant places in Osaka with plenty of activity during the day and night. From the famous Glico runner to the Kani Dōraku crab, everything here is designed to attract the eye.

Step outside the main street and the urban chaos continues with lights, signage and a web of cables, a classic sight of urban Japan.
