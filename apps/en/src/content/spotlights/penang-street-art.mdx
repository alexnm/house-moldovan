George Town turned its back lanes into an open-air gallery — murals that spill off the wall and into the street, using real bicycles, swings and motorbikes as props. Ernest Zacharevic's interactive pieces anchor the circuit, but the hunt through the old quarter rewards you with cats, children and peeling walls at every turn.
