From the square, Uzbekistan is domes and portals. Up close it is glaze: cut tiles, painted vaults, and brick left bare where the pattern stops. These are the details we kept walking back to in **Khiva**, **Bukhara** and **Samarkand** on our [2019 Silk Road trip](/itineraries/uzbekistan-silk-road).

Our favorite spot was probably Shah-i-Zinda, in Samarkand. It's an alley of mausoleums where every facade is more interesting than the last one. On top of that, Bukhara and Khiva offer many mosques and madrasas to enjoy the intricate patterns of tilework on their portals.
