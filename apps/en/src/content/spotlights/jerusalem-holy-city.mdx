We spent three days in Jerusalem during our [10-day trip through Israel and Jordan](/itineraries/israel-jordan-10-days). It was enough to walk every quarter of the Old City, climb the walls and wonder around the hills surrounding the walled town. Jerusalem has so many layers of history and religion that it's almost impossible to capture it in just a few photos, but here's out best try.
